Our philosophy

School taught you many subjects. Rarely the one you use every day: how to decide.

Big life transitions shouldn't be navigated alone, and they can't be solved by a one-size-fits-all online course.

So we built a school without a fixed syllabus. Your studies begin the moment you face a real dilemma. Each time, you are matched with a tutor whose expertise fits your situation, and you leave with an answer and with skills you keep for the next decision.

    Questions

    Before you enrol

    Is there a fixed curriculum?

    No. Your studies are built around the decisions you bring. When you submit a dilemma, your tutor prepares guidance and a reading list for that situation only.

    How long does my enrolment last?

    For life. You enrol once, and you keep the right to submit new challenges whenever you face them. There are no renewals or monthly fees.

    Who are the tutors?

    A curated network of educators with expertise across careers, relationships, finance, ethics, wellbeing and more. Each inquiry is assigned to the one whose experience fits your situation.

    Is what I share kept private?

    Yes. All interactions are strictly confidential and take place only between you and your assigned tutor.
